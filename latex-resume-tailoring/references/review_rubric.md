# Resume Tailoring Review Rubric

Use this rubric for the final review and for any borderline tailoring decision.

## ATS Reviewer

Check whether the tailored resume contains natural, parseable evidence for the JD's core requirements:

- Required technologies and hard skills appear in relevant experience, projects, or skills sections.
- Role focus is clear within the top third of the resume.
- Section headings and dates remain parser-friendly.
- Keywords are distributed where they are supported by evidence, not dumped into a single list.
- Important JD terms are not replaced only by obscure synonyms.

Report remaining ATS gaps as unsupported gaps, not as defects to fabricate around.

## HR Recruiter

Judge whether a recruiter can identify fit in a quick scan:

- Recent or strongest experience aligns with the target role.
- Bullets expose business or user impact where verified.
- The resume avoids dense, generic, or overly academic wording.
- The candidate's seniority signal matches the JD without exaggeration.
- Projects chosen for emphasis are understandable without deep technical context.

## Senior SDE Interviewer

Evaluate whether the resume earns technical confidence:

- Technical claims are specific enough to drive interview questions.
- Architecture, scale, reliability, performance, data, security, or product tradeoffs appear where supported.
- Project bullets show implementation depth, not just tool name-dropping.
- Metrics are credible and tied to the user's own contribution.
- Claims do not imply ownership, production deployment, or leadership beyond the vault.

## Quality and Integrity

Flag any issue that could reduce trust:

- Hallucination risk: any claim not clearly supported by the career vault.
- Keyword stuffing risk: repeated or awkward JD terms that disrupt natural bullets.
- Format drift risk: template, spacing, section order, macro, or tone changes that were not necessary.
- Vague inflation: broad claims without evidence, context, or result.
- Remaining JD gaps: important requirements with no verified match.

For each meaningful modification, be able to explain why it was necessary for relevance, clarity, ATS coverage, or one-page fit.
